Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

MD Files Uploader ("the app") is a private tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel MD Files to upload video clips to that channel. It is not available to the public.

Google user data the app accesses

When the owner signs in with Google, the app requests these permissions:

  • youtube.upload: to upload videos to the owner's YouTube channel.
  • youtube.readonly: to read basic information about the owner's own channel and its videos.

How the data is used and stored

  • The data is used only to upload and schedule videos on the owner's own channel.
  • The sign-in token is stored only on the owner's own computer. It is not sent to any other server.
  • No personal data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • The app does not collect data from anyone other than the owner.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

YouTube API Services

This app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. See also the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from Google Account permissions. Deleting the stored token on the owner's computer also removes the app's access.
